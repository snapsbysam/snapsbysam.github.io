About the Artist

Hello! I'm Hana, an oil painter with a deep passion for creating works that evoke emotion and beauty. I specialize in landscape and portrait oil paintings, using simple colours and rich textures to bring the subject to life. Each painting is a reflection of my love for art and the world around me. I believe in capturing the soul of nature and people through brushstrokes, turning ordinary moments into extraordinary ones.
